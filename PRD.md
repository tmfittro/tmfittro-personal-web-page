# Product Requirement Document

## 01 — Problem Statement

Taryn Fittro is a senior at the University of Iowa studying Business Analytics and Information Systems, graduating in May 2026. She is seeking roles that combine data analytics with the real estate and housing industry, with interests in supply chain, forecasting, and operations.

A traditional resume alone does not effectively communicate her technical skills, project experience, and ability to apply analytics to real-world business problems. This landing page will serve as a centralized, professional digital presence that showcases her work, skills, and career direction in a more dynamic and accessible way.

---

## 02 — Target Audience

The primary audience for this landing page includes recruiters, hiring managers, and professionals in industries such as real estate, consulting, and analytics.

More specifically:
- Real estate firms and investment groups (Chicago, Boston, Silicon Valley)
- Consulting firms and analytics-driven organizations
- Hiring managers looking for entry-level analysts with technical and business skills

These visitors are looking to quickly understand:
- Who Taryn is
- What skills she has
- What projects she has completed
- Whether she is a strong candidate for analytics-related roles
